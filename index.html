The house

Three rooms. One ground.

Vanasthali means a forest grove — many trees, shared earth. Students move between temple classical, festival folk and the quiet of yoga without flattening any tradition.

Classical

Eight recognised forms

Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Odissi, Kuchipudi, Kathakali, Mohiniyattam, Manipuri, Sattriya.
Folk & stage

Festival and film

Garba, Bhangra, Ghoomar, Lavani, Bihu, Chhau, Kalbelia, Bollywood, hip hop, contemporary.
Yoga

Breath before spectacle

Hatha, Vinyasa, Ashtanga, Yin, pranayama, meditation, and dancer-specific mobility.

How one begins

Trial. Placement. Term.

A complimentary trial, then a level batch for twelve weeks. Yoga may be taken alone or paired with dance.

01

Watch a class

Sit in or take the trial. No costume required — only comfortable clothes.

02

Find your batch

Kids, teens, adult beginners or performance crew. Morning yoga, evening dance.

03

Stay for the term

Twelve weeks, graded work, optional utsav and festival sets.
